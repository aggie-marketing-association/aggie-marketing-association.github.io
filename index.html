Our Purpose

About Us

AMA is a student-led, student-controlled organization dedicated to strategic brand communication, consumer insight analysis, and marketing analytics.

Unlike general business organizations, we specifically serve students in communication, economics, and related fields.

We prepare students for careers by hosting skill-building workshops, connecting them with alumni and industry professionals, and facilitating corporate partnerships for real-world project experience and recruitment exposure.

What We Do

Events & Workshops

A quarterly calendar built around practical skills, member discussion, and direct contact with the industry.

Workshops

Skill-Building Workshops

Hands-on sessions on brand strategy, consumer research, and marketing analytics, led by upperclassmen and alumni working in the field.

Bi-weekly · 90 minutes

Meetings

General Meetings

Low-commitment meetings where members share project updates, meet one another, and plan the quarter together.

Monthly · All members

Partnerships

Corporate Partnerships

Real client projects and recruitment exposure through partnerships with agencies, marketing teams, and alumni-founded companies.

Project-based · One quarter

Time commitment General membership is designed to fit a full course load — under 4 hours per week.
